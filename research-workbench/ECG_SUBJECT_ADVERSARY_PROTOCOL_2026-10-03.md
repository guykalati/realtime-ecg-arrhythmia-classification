# Subject-adversarial development objective — frozen 3 October 2026

Purpose: test the second planned ECG method after past-only timing improved S but left F poorly detected. Source basis and limitations are in ECG_NEXT_METHODS_RESEARCH_2026-10-03.md (Zhang 2021); this is a modified objective test, not a reproduction or clinical claim.

Keep the existing centered waveform CNN plus three past-only timing features. Use the exact same two subject-grouped MIT partitions and three seeds, transformations fit on MIT training only, 12 epochs, square-root class weights, AdamW lr 0.001/decay 0.0001, lowest unweighted subject-disjoint validation CE checkpoint. The existing target/context subsets and all source/array/timing/split hashes remain fixed. MIT records 201/202 stay in one documented group. INCART is scored only as inspected external development, never fitted. EDB and SVDB remain unscored/sealed respectively.

Add a training-only linear classifier over the 64-dimensional waveform embedding predicting actual MIT training subject groups. Reverse its encoder gradient with weights 0, 0.05 and 0.2; keep the subject-head gradient ordinary and its cross-entropy unweighted. The zero-weight control trains an otherwise inert head and preserves the rhythm classifier/encoder objective. No subject gradient reaches timing features. Subject loss plus weighted rhythm loss are optimized jointly. Fit no subject head on validation/test/INCART. Record subject class support, dominant-group fraction, subject losses/accuracy and validation history.

Run 2 splits × 3 seeds × 3 weights = 18 cells. Identical initial rhythm logits and training shuffles across weights. A meaningful preflight selfcheck checks initial-logit identity, reversed encoder gradient sign/scale, ordinary subject-head gradients and zero-weight control. Choose the weight by lowest mean unweighted validation CE across the six matched cells, tie favoring smaller weight. Do not select using MIT test or INCART. Report every arm.

For the validation-selected nonzero candidate to become a provisional development reference, require positive mean macro-F1 and S-F1 deltas on both development sources, nondecreasing F recall/precision and nonincreasing Brier on both, and mean S/F false-positive counts no more than 110% of control on either source. These are exploratory workflow safeguards, not clinical targets or significance thresholds. Subject suppression can remove genuine rare-class signal concentrated in a few people; failure retains the current combined model. Six cells share only two partitions and are not six independent cohorts.

One RTX3090 allocation, 15 minutes; 780-second internal run/epoch cap, 840-second external timeout, 80 MB output cap. No retries/new data/downloads/EDB or SVDB access. Check every reloaded validation score and checkpoint hash. Publish compact results only, preserve large arrays/checkpoints externally. Classification still uses annotated peaks and 0.5-second future waveform latency; no prospective-monitor claim.
