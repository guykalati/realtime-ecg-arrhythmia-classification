Three modes: existing centered waveform CNN, timing-only3→16→4 MLP, and waveform embedding plus three timing features. Combined head starts with the same waveform weights/bias and zero timing weights, verified by a synthetic initial-logit comparison. New timesteps are not neighboring waveform channels.
